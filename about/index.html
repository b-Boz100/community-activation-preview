Introducing CoAct

For 29 years, CoAct has united a network of not-for-profit community service providers, collaborating to deliver employment and community services across Australia. Today, that network includes 12 service partners, creating impact across over 500 sites nationally.

Our philosophy

At CoAct, our purpose is simple but powerful: to help people create positive, lasting change in their lives and communities.

We believe everyone has the potential to contribute purposefully. By bringing together the power of our people, partners and communities, we create opportunities and outcomes that no one could achieve alone.

We meet people where they are, helping them grow, build connection and access the support they need to move forward.

Growing the network matters less to us than who’s in it. We look for partners who share our values, not simply ones who add scale or efficiency. That alignment is what makes collaboration and shared best practice actually work across the network, creating value no single organisation could build on its own. Every partner keeps the local knowledge and relationships that make them valuable to their community.

Our why stays the same: helping people create positive, lasting change and activating communities for meaningful outcomes.

Our network of NFP community partners

CoAct’s not-for-profit partners work hand in hand with us to deliver employment services in local communities across Australia.

29 years of building, innovation and investment to drive community activation

Our forward focus, shared investment and collaboration help our community partners embed successfully in their local communities, delivering employment and community services.

Together, our network brings the scale, capability and community connections to deliver meaningful outcomes:

  • A reputable national employment services provider: delivering government contracts on behalf of more than 30,000 jobseekers, worth $120 million in contract revenue.
  • A strong and vibrant network: 12 not-for-profit community partners with a combined revenue of more than $1 billion.
  • A winning track record: eighth-largest Workforce Australia provider in the country, with a national partner network that has grown through successive procurement cycles since 2018.
  • Proven models and infrastructure: $7.5 million invested annually in supporting partners, and over $3 million invested in technology.
  • Deep experience: over 100 years of combined senior leadership and industry knowledge across the network. Average partner tenure - 9.6 years.
  • A trusted partner to government: known as a safe pair of hands by the department.
  • A responsive, national footprint: 506 people delivering services from 562 sites across Australia in FY26.
