Case study

Five Bridges: first Workforce Australia contract, compliant from day one

Five Bridges is a First Nations community organisation in Queensland. With the CoAct network behind it, Five Bridges won and began delivering Workforce Australia Indigenous specialist contracts in Fitzroy and Brisbane South. It was the organisation’s first employment services contract.

The challenge

Federally funded employment services come with a heavy load before a single client is seen: the Deed, program guidelines, reporting, claims and performance measures. For a small organisation entering for the first time, that’s a lot to learn at once.

What CoAct did

CoAct supported the procurement and set up the contracts within the required timeframe. The network shared its knowledge of the program, its compliance systems and its reporting processes, and supported Five Bridges on the administrative and operational side of delivery, including performance measures.

The result

Five Bridges supported Indigenous clients from day one of the contract. Its caseload has grown 25% since commencement, and it has made 1,896 placements.
