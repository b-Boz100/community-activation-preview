Case study: Five Bridges

Delivering results for Five Bridges

Five Bridges - a community organisation that grew into Workforce Australia for the first time, protected from day one by compliance systems the network already had in place.

CoAct’s knowledge and experience in employment services was invaluable to Five Bridges in ensuring their successful entry into delivering the Workforce Australia Indigenous specialist contracts.

The challenge

For small community organisations, stepping into the domain of federally funded programs, such as employment services, is often fraught with complexity. The CoAct network facilitated a successful procurement and entry into employment services for the first time for community organisation Five Bridges. Our substantial support and systems allowed them to support Indigenous clients in Workforce Australia efficiently and effectively from day one of the new contract.

Key achievements

Speedy contract establishment: CoAct’s robust support system enabled Five Bridges to secure their contracts swiftly and within the stipulated time frame, adhering strictly to the conditions in the Deed and the program delivery guidelines.

Expertise and knowledge transfer: CoAct’s long-standing expertise in federally funded programs empowered Five Bridges. They were fast-tracked in their comprehension of the nuanced facets of the employment services program, ensuring that they met and exceeded the standards and expectations set out by the funding agreement.

Holistic support: CoAct’s involvement wasn’t limited to mere consultation. They provided comprehensive assistance, extending to insights into the administrative and operational aspects of the program, ensuring compliance, understanding performance metrics, and meeting the rigorous reporting requirements.

The CoAct network’s depth of knowledge and experience proved indispensable to Five Bridges. Their involvement guaranteed that this community organisation not only entered but thrived in delivering on the mandates of the Workforce Australia contract – a strong proof point for community organisations weighing up their own first step into employment services.
