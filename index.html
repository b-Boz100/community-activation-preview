Reform is coming. The decisions start now.

Workforce Australia will be replaced by a new employment services system, targeted to start on 1 November 2028. The procurement that decides who delivers it will come well before then.

For small and mid-sized not-for-profits, that means four pressures at once. You need to grow before the market consolidates, carry a heavier compliance load, meet a higher performance bar, and find the scale to manage all three.

You keep your identity. We bring the rest.

CoAct partners keep their name, their local relationships and their knowledge of the communities they serve. The network provides procurement, service design, financial modelling, governance and compliance, technology, data, workforce capability, employer relationships and government engagement.

CoAct is currently the eighth-largest Workforce Australia provider and the fifth-largest Inclusive Employment Australia (IEA) provider – and the largest specialist IEA provider in the country – with its national partner network continuing to grow through successive government employment services procurement cycles since 2018.

We invest $7.5 million each year in supporting our partners, including more than $3 million in technology. Average partner tenure across the network is 9.6 years.
