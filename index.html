CoAct CEO foreword

Dear prospective partner,

The next chapter of Workforce Australia is taking shape now.

With the new system commencing on 1 November 2028, the decisions made over the coming months will decide which organisations are positioned to succeed in the future employment services market. For established not-for-profit providers, particularly smaller and mid-sized organisations, the procurement ahead means four things at once: growing your position before the market consolidates further, protecting yourself from a rising compliance load, delivering to a higher performance bar, and finding the scale to do all three without building it alone.

At CoAct, we believe you shouldn’t have to face that alone.

For 29 years, CoAct has brought together not-for-profit organisations to combine the strength of a network with the local knowledge and relationships that make each organisation valuable to its community.

As a CoAct partner, you keep your organisation’s identity, local relationships and understanding of the communities you serve. The network gives you everything else: procurement, service design, financial modelling, governance and compliance, technology, data, performance, workforce capability, employer relationships and government engagement.

CoAct is currently the eighth-largest Workforce Australia provider and the fifth-largest Inclusive Employment Australia (IEA) provider - and the largest specialist IEA provider in the country - with its national partner network continuing to grow through successive government employment services procurement cycles since 2018.

We invest $7.5 million each year in supporting our partners, including more than $3 million in technology. Average partner tenure across the network is 9.6 years. But the real value of the network isn’t its size. It’s what partners can do with that collective strength.

CoAct is now speaking with a select number of established not-for-profit providers considering their future in Workforce Australia. We’re not looking simply to grow the number of organisations in our network - we’re looking for partners whose capability, community connections and geographic presence complement what already exists. Partners who share our values get more from the network than the numbers alone show: the collaboration, the shared best practice, and the value that only comes from genuinely combining resources rather than just adding them up.

The new Workforce Australia system may not commence until 2028, but the decisions shaping it start much sooner. Now is the time to understand your options and decide whether CoAct could be the right network for your organisation.

Simon BrownChief Executive Officer, CoAct
