Enterprise-grade technology, procurement power and insight are out of reach for a single mid-size provider – but not for a network of twelve.

What the network gives you:

Digital transformation
access to a secure digital ecosystem that reduces the time, resources and investment required to build and maintain technology.
Network-scale procurement
a negotiated discount on JobReady licensing, plus access to Campus – CoAct’s own customer platform, backed by more than $500k invested across the last two Workforce Australia and Inclusive Employment Australia procurements – savings and infrastructure no partner could build alone.
Behavioural science insights for client management
the OPUS Assessment, developed with behavioural scientist Dr Darren Coppin, looks beyond a client’s circumstances to the behavioural drivers – action orientation, future optimism, confidence in the job search process – that actually predict employment success, so consultants can target support earlier.
Network collaboration
opportunities to benchmark, share ideas and learn from like-minded organisations in a non-competitive environment.
